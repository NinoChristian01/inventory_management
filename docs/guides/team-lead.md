# Team Lead Guide

Team Leads oversee ticket handling for their team, coordinate assignments, monitor progress, and support agents while maintaining service quality.

## Getting started
1. **Sign in** at `/account/login`.
2. Complete a password change if prompted.
3. Use the **Dashboard** (`/`) to monitor open, unassigned, overdue, and recently updated tickets within your access scope.

## What you can do

### Monitor and manage ticket flow
- Use **Tickets** (`/tickets`) to triage, search, filter, and prioritize team-related work.
- Open ticket details to review requester context, conversation, attachments, activity, SLA due dates, and watchers.
- Visibility is scoped by permissions/access rules (typically team-level visibility for Team Leads).

### Coordinate assignment and workflow
- **Assign/reassign tickets** (when permitted) to balance workload and ensure ownership.
- **Update ticket status/workflow** (when permitted), including progression through pending/resolved states according to process.
- Ensure resolutions include clear summaries and appropriate follow-up communication.

### Collaborate with agents and requesters
- Post **public replies** to communicate with requesters.
- Add **internal notes** (when permitted) for team coordination and handoffs.
- Upload attachments and review submitted files.
- **Watch** key tickets to stay informed via in-app notifications.

### User and team visibility
- Access **Users** (`/users`) to view/search users in scope.
  - Administrative actions such as activation/deactivation and role changes may be restricted to Admins in the current UI.
- Access **Teams** (`/teams`) to view team/department structure, routing context, and membership counts.

### Notifications
- Use **Notifications** (`/notifications`) to track assignment changes, replies, status updates, and other relevant ticket events.
- Mark notifications read individually or in bulk.

## Operational guidance
- Review unassigned and overdue tickets regularly from the dashboard/ticket list.
- Use internal notes for escalation context and continuity during handoffs.
- Encourage consistent categorization, prioritization, and resolution documentation to improve reporting and SLA performance.
- Verify that team members have appropriate access/roles (coordinate with Admins for account/role changes).

## Current limitations (may vary by deployment)
- Some Team Lead capabilities depend on configured permissions and current implementation.
- Full admin/reference-data management (catalogs, SLA policies, business hours/holidays, role/permission administration, audit/recycle-bin tools), advanced reporting/exports, and automated SLA/escalation jobs may be partially implemented or Admin-only.
